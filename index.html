Start with one tape

Privacy-first. We never resell your footage.

Three passes on every tape.

Every playback wears a tape a little more, so we work to get it right the first time.

  • Every frame, tape or film

    Video tape is captured at its own frame rate, with nothing dropped or blended. Super 8 film is scanned one frame at a time, then played back at the speed it was shot.

  • Each tape’s color, set by hand

    One of us watches every scene and corrects the faded reds, the green casts and the washed-out skies. We don’t run one filter over the whole tape.

  • Files that outlast the tape

    You get files any phone, laptop or TV plays today, in formats you’ll still be able to open in twenty years. Your tapes come back to you too.
